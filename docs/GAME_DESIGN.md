# Game design — 《瑟瓦納：森林的抉擇》

A calm, illustrated forest exploration RPG punctuated by turn-based encounters. A fictional traveler follows a field notebook through a fog-covered forest. Rather than defeating a cultural figure, the player confronts original manifestations of waste and haste.

Loop: explore a location → talk/observe/collect → optionally help with environmental actions → confront an original enemy → choose with reasons → advance. The HUD keeps the next objective visible. Rest and repeatable training prevent soft locks; death recovers the player without losing quest progress.

Six discovery skills (WHO/WHERE/WHEN/WHAT/WHY/HOW) organize evidence. Reflection in combat reads already-discovered clues and can expose a weakness; basic combat never needs a quiz. Three dilemmas record choice, reason and consequence; all choices remain playable. Nine original environmental quests fill a 3×3 action grid. The ending asks for a concrete personal pledge and reflects the player's choices.

Visual direction: warm parchment on a midnight-green forest, hand-built SVG scenery, amber quest markers, a visible traveler, layered foliage and drifting mist. No culturally specific costumes or sacred iconography.

Pacing: eight locations/chapters, nine optional quests, three ordinary enemy types plus an original boss, max level 10. Estimated first completion: 15–30 minutes.
